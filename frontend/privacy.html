Privacy Policy

SST Live is a student project that shows live scores for SST inter-house tournaments. Watching scores needs no account.

What we use Google sign-in for

When you sign in with Google we read only your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to show who you are in the top bar and in chat, and to decide what you are allowed to do. We do not read your contacts, Drive, Gmail or anything else.

Who can sign in

Only @sst.scaler.com and @scaler.com accounts can sign in. Any other Google account is turned away.

Where your data is stored

Your name, email and picture are stored in a MongoDB Atlas database, only to run the app. We do not sell or share them and we do not use them for ads.

What other people can see

Emails, roll numbers and batches are never shown publicly. Only admins can see account emails. In chat, only the part of your email before the "@" is shown to other users.

Deleting your account

You can ask the admin to delete your account and the data stored with it.
